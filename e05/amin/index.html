Amazing Features

Snowdrop is packed with features and possibilities, making it easy to share everything you have on your phone with your friends and family.

  • Open Source App is developed using some of the best security protocols. On top of that, it's open-source, so developers are continuously improving it.
  • Cross Platform Snowdrop works in your browser. A bit like Apple's AirDrop, but not only for Apple devices. Windows, Linux, Android, iPhone, Mac - no problem at all!
  • Easy to Use Everything is pretty straightforward. As long as you have both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network, you can access Snowdrop and send your files.
